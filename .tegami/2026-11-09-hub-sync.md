### ✨ Sync plans between machines through the hub

Enrolled machines now share events, plan files and their revisions through the hub, encrypted and signed on the machine that wrote them: every plans write and session start syncs in the background for up to two seconds, `skills hub sync` runs a full pass and prints what moved, and `skills hub status` shows the queue, held, fetching and conflict counts. An edit made on two machines at once stays on both disks as a conflict that `skills plans show --conflict` prints, and a restored or replaced hub is reconciled on the next sync.
